## Why the site is typographic rather than photo-led

Stock "phone on a desk" photography is the strongest tell of a template site. The layout carries the brand with
type, rules and a paper palette instead, and uses one art-directed greyscale band for texture. If you want more
photography later, the honest upgrade is your own: your counter, your team, a device being graded, a payout
happening. That reads as a real business in a way licensed stock never will.
