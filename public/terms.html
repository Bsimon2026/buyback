05 Inspection and our right to refuse

Every device is inspected before we buy it. Inspection is free of charge and takes roughly ten minutes. It covers nine condition points — screen, body, battery health, camera, speaker, Face ID or fingerprint, charging, water damage and repair history — plus the IMEI or serial number, which we check against our purchase records.

We may decline to buy a device, and we may end an inspection at any time, if:

  • the device is stolen, blocked, or subject to a third-party claim;
  • it carries an activation lock or a device-management profile you cannot remove;
  • it is not as described, or the IMEI or serial number does not match the device presented;
  • identification cannot be verified, or the person presenting the device is not entitled to sell it;
  • we reasonably suspect fraud or money laundering.

Refusal costs you nothing and we will return the device to you immediately.

06 How the final price is set

Each model has a base buy price published on this website. The inspection result then applies standard adjustments for the condition points above. The same rules that produced your online estimate produce the final offer, and every adjustment is shown to you item by item before you decide.

If an inspector proposes a figure different from the calculated result, that is an exception, it requires a written reason, and it is recorded against your order. You can see the reasoning on request.

08 Ownership, locks and blocked devices

By selling a device you confirm that it is your property, that no other person has a claim over it, and that it is not subject to a financing plan, insurance claim, lease or rental agreement.

Removing locks is your responsibility. Before we collect a phone, sign out of the manufacturer account (for example “Find My iPhone”) and remove any screen lock you do not want to disclose; for a laptop, remove device-management (MDM) profiles and any BIOS or firmware password. If a lock prevents us from testing or erasing the device, we may decline the purchase or revise the price to reflect it.

If we later establish that a device was stolen, blocked, or sold without authority, we may report it to the police, and you agree to repay the amount paid plus any reasonable costs we incur. We also co-operate with law-enforcement requests concerning devices we have purchased.

09 Your data

Back up anything you want to keep before you hand the device over. Once we take custody of a device, the data on it cannot be returned to you.

You choose how your data is treated when you book, and you can change your choice before handover:

  • Erase and resell — all user-addressable storage is erased with certified software to a recognised standard, the completion log is checked, and the device is inspected before it is offered for resale.
  • Destroy the storage — the storage media is physically destroyed, so recovery of any data from it is not possible. The device itself is then sent for material recovery.

We issue a certificate of data sanitisation for the chosen treatment, naming the method, the standard applied, the operator and the date. It is generated from our order record, not typed by hand.

We do not read, copy, index or retain the contents of your device. Where storage media cannot be erased successfully, it is destroyed instead, and the certificate records that outcome.

We do not promise that data recovery is impossible in every conceivable circumstance, and we are not liable for data you lose. What we commit to is the method above, applied and recorded. Our handling of personal data is described in our privacy policy.

10 Devices we do not buy

If a device has no resale value, we will usually still accept it for recycling at a licensed facility for material recovery. No payment is made for scrap-value devices, but the data treatment you chose still applies and a certificate is still issued.

We do not accept devices containing gases, liquids, radioactive material or batteries in a damaged or swollen state. Tell us before handover if a device is in that condition.
